# Design taste

- Rejects generic "modern SaaS" UI clichés: purple/indigo "AI" gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, glowing borders, generic dashboards, and effects added just because they are trendy. Confidence: 0.9
- Wants a distinctive, premium, "expensive-feeling" identity, not a collage of components copied from several sites; the whole surface must read as one coherent product. Confidence: 0.85
- Treats restraint as part of premium design — prefers less decoration, and animation only where it communicates hierarchy, state, or cause and effect rather than moving everything. Confidence: 0.85
- Expects the agent to choose all visual specifics itself (color, typography, spacing, radius, motion, layout, component set) rather than asking the user, who does not consider themselves a visual designer. Confidence: 0.9
